TeaTea Privacy Policy

TeaTea (hereinafter referred to as the "App") strives to handle information properly and protect user privacy in order to deliver supportive messages to users.
The App does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as names or email addresses.

1. Information Collected and Used

The information collected and used by the App is as follows:

  • Information Entered/Configured by Users: Notification times (morning, noon, night)
  • Information Automatically Generated/Stored: Theme information required for message generation (time period, season, etc.)
  • Information Automatically Collected by External Tools: Advertising IDs, device identifiers, IP addresses, OS versions, device models, app operation/viewing logs, crash data, etc. (automatically collected via external services described in Sections 5 and 6 below)

2. Storage of Data

Notification times configured by users and generated messages are stored in local storage on the device (SharedPreferences).
This information is managed solely within the user's device and is not directly accessible by the developer.

3. External Transmission of Data

Minimal data (non-personally identifiable information), such as theme information, is transmitted to an external server for message generation processing.
The transmitted data contains no personally identifiable information such as names or email addresses.

4. Provision to Third Parties

The App will not provide acquired information to third parties without user consent, except as required by law.

5. Ad Delivery

The App serves advertisements provided by Google LLC, a third-party distributor (Google AdMob).
Accordingly, providers such as Google may automatically collect and use device identifiers (advertising IDs, etc.), IP addresses, and viewing/operation log data.
This information is used for optimal ad delivery, measuring ad effectiveness, and fraud prevention.
For details on information handling and opting out of personalized advertising, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Usage Analysis and Crash Reporting

To improve service quality, enhance performance, and resolve bugs, the App utilizes "Firebase" (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, etc.) provided by Google LLC.
Consequently, app usage data (screen views, operation logs, crash data during errors, etc.), IP addresses, and device information may be automatically transmitted and analyzed.
This data is collected anonymously and does not identify individuals.

  • Firebase Terms of Service: https://firebase.google.com/terms

7. Data Deletion Method

By uninstalling the App from your device, all setting data and cache stored on the device will be deleted.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised without prior notice due to changes in laws and regulations or additions/modifications to App features.
